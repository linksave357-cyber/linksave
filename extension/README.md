# LinkSave Browser Extension (Chrome & Edge)

A lightweight browser extension that adds a **"Download with LinkSave"** button directly under YouTube and Instagram videos, and provides an instant toolbar popup to download any video in HD MP4 or MP3.

---

## Features

- **YouTube Integration**: Injects a clean `Download` button right next to the Like/Share buttons on YouTube watch pages and Shorts.
- **Instagram Reels Integration**: Injects a `Save Reel` button directly onto Instagram reels and video posts.
- **Auto-Detect Popup**: Click the LinkSave extension in your browser toolbar to automatically detect the current video and jump straight to download.
- **Zero Configuration**: Works immediately without sign-up or API keys.

---

## How to Install in Chrome, Edge, or Brave (30 Seconds)

1. Open your browser and navigate to the Extensions page:
   - **Chrome**: `chrome://extensions/`
   - **Edge**: `edge://extensions/`
   - **Brave**: `brave://extensions/`
2. In the top-right corner, toggle **Developer mode** to **ON**.
3. Click the **"Load unpacked"** button in the top-left corner.
4. Select the `extension` folder located at:
   ```text
   c:\my-projects\linksave\extension
   ```
5. Pin **LinkSave** to your browser toolbar:
   - Click the puzzle icon (Extensions) in your browser toolbar.
   - Click the pin icon next to **LinkSave**.

---

## How to Test

1. Open any YouTube video (e.g. `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...`).
2. You will see a blue gradient **"Download"** button right next to the Like and Share buttons.
3. Click it — it will open LinkSave with your video link pre-filled and ready to convert!
4. Open any Instagram Reel — click the **"Save Reel"** button to download.
