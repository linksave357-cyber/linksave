1. Downloading on iPhone & iPad (iOS Safari)

Apple iOS 13 and later includes a built-in Safari download manager:

  1. Open YouTube and copy the video URL from the Share menu.
  2. Open Safari and visit LinkSave.
  3. Paste the URL and tap Download. Safari will prompt: "Do you want to download this file?"
  4. Tap Download. The file saves inside your iPhone's Files > Downloads folder.

2. Downloading on Android (Google Chrome & Firefox)

Android devices save files straight to local storage:

  1. Copy the video URL from the YouTube app.
  2. Paste it into LinkSave on Google Chrome.
  3. Tap your desired MP4 resolution or MP3 audio format. The file will immediately download to your phone gallery or My Files folder.

3. Downloading on Desktop PCs (Windows & Mac)

On desktop web browsers (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox):

  1. Highlight the address bar URL of the video and press Ctrl + C (or Cmd + C).
  2. Paste the link into LinkSave and click Download.
  3. The media file will land in your computer's default Downloads folder.

4. Audio Bitrate Deep Dive: 320kbps vs 256kbps vs 128kbps

Audio bitrates determine the fidelity and sample clarity of MP3 files extracted from online streams:

320 kbps (Ultra HQ)

Maximum acoustic quality. Preserves instrument separation and deep bass for studio monitors and headphones.

256 kbps (High Quality)

Near-indistinguishable quality for standard speakers with reduced file sizes.

128 kbps (Standard)

Compact filesize ideal for podcasts, audiobooks, and low-bandwidth connections.
